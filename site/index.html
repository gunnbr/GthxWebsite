What gthx does

Factoids

Teach it something once and anyone can ask for it later. Values can be replies, actions, or several answers joined together.

gthx: extruder is the part that pushes the plastic

Messages

Leave a message for someone who isn’t around. gthx passes it on the next time they speak.

gthx: tell someuser the parts arrived

Reminders

Relative times from a minute to a year ahead. They survive restarts.

gthx: remind me in 2 hours to check the print

Seen

When and where someone last spoke, and what they said.

seen someuser?

Link titles

YouTube, Thingiverse, Printables and MakerWorld links get a title and a count of how often they’ve come up.

https://youtu.be/<id>

Status

Version, uptime and mood. Feed it a botsnack and its mood improves.
